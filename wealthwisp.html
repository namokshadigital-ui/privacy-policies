📋 1. Overview

Welcome to WealthWisp — Personal Finance Tracker & Budget Manager (package name: com.rn.namoksha.wealthwisp), developed and published by Namoksha Digital ("we", "our", or "us"). Available on Google Play Store. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our Android application.

WealthWisp is a personal finance app that helps you track your income, expenses, and budget goals — designed with privacy in mind.

Key principle: Your financial data (transactions, budgets, accounts) is stored locally on your device. We do not upload your financial records to our servers.

By using WealthWisp, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy. If you disagree with any part, please discontinue use of the App.

📊 2. Data We Collect

2.1 Data You Provide

  • Financial Data: The income, expenses, accounts, and budgets you enter are stored locally in the App's database on your device.
  • App Preferences: Settings such as currency, language, and theme choices are stored locally.

2.2 Data Collected Automatically

  • Crash Reports: If the App crashes, Firebase Crashlytics collects crash stack traces and device info (OS version, device model) to help us fix bugs.
  • Analytics Events: Firebase Analytics collects anonymized usage events (e.g., app launches, screen views) to help us improve the App's features.
  • Ad Identifiers: Google AdMob may collect your advertising ID to serve personalized or non-personalized ads, subject to your consent.
  • Device Information: Approximate device type, OS version, language, and timezone — collected by Firebase and AdMob for analytics and ad delivery.

2.3 Data We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect or upload your financial transaction history to our servers.
  • We do not require you to create an account, so we do not collect your name, email, or passwords.
  • We do not collect your bank account details or credit card numbers.
  • We do not sell your personal data to any third party.

⚙️ 3. How We Use Your Data

  • To provide and improve App functionality (tracking finances and generating reports).
  • To diagnose and fix crashes using Firebase Crashlytics.
  • To understand user engagement patterns using Firebase Analytics.
  • To display advertisements through Google AdMob.
All financial tracking, categorization, and report generation happens 100% on-device.

🤝 6. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information. We may share data only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Third-party service providers: Google (AdMob, Firebase) as described above, solely to operate the App.
  • Legal requirements: If required by law, court order, or governmental authority.
  • Business transfers: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, user data may be transferred as a business asset.

🗂️ 7. Data Retention

  • Financial Data: Stored on your device until you delete the data within the App or uninstall the App.
  • App preferences: Stored locally on your device via Android DataStore.
  • Crash reports (Crashlytics): Retained by Firebase for 90 days.
  • Analytics data (Firebase): Retained for up to 14 months, then automatically deleted.
  • Ad data (AdMob): Subject to Google's retention policies.
